## Production notes

Preview only. No schedule changes or publishing.

Single and carousel: 1080 × 1350, paper texture and consistent Georgia Italic typography.
Video: 1080 × 1920, 12 seconds, slow camera zoom on an original AI-generated nature poster. Silent; this is not live nature footage.

Suggested starting mix at the existing one-post-per-day cadence: Monday/Thursday carousel, Tuesday/Friday reel, Wednesday/Saturday/Sunday single. This is a proposal, not a claim that one format guarantees growth.
