# LDS Conferences — source-grounded engagement strategy

Publish daily at 04:20 Asia/Kuala_Lumpur. Weekly mix: Monday, Thursday and
Sunday carousel; Tuesday and Friday reel; Wednesday and Saturday single post.

Content helps people understand, apply and discuss official General Conference
messages. It does not summarise talks merely to fill a feed. Each post uses one
relatable tension, one source-grounded insight, one realistic application and
one sincere question. Avoid comment bait, private-confession prompts, controversy,
doctrinal invention, guilt and hard app promotion.

Only configured English General Conference pages on ChurchofJesusChrist.org are
accepted. The full official transcript is fetched for each post. Media and captions
use editorial paraphrase rather than direct quotations and label themselves as
independent study reflections. A separate Claude source-grounding review must pass.

Carousels contain six concise slides. Singles contain one memorable reflection.
Reels contain four six-second scenes over unused portrait Pexels nature footage,
with a dark contrast layer and gentle original instrumental audio. No copyrighted
song, Conference recording, synthetic voice or paid video generation is used.
Pexels creator and source links appear in reel captions.

Media is temporarily hosted in a dedicated GitHub release with a seven-day cleanup
window, 25 MiB per-file cap, 80-asset cap and 350 MiB total cap. Pexels uses at most
six searches per reel. Provider IDs are permanently reserved for live Conference
posts and kept separate from LDS Quotes. Different IDs may still depict similar scenes.

At most two model calls per post: Haiku draft and Sonnet source review. Failed
validation alerts Slack and stops. Buffer submission is date-idempotent and unknown
delivery outcomes are never automatically reposted. A 04:50 delivery check reports
missing or failed runs without publishing.
