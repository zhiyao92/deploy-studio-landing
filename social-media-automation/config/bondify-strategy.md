# Bondify — research-led editorial strategy

Approved direction: 2026-09-08. Research-led runtime implemented 2026-09-09. Firestore runtime fields record deployment activation and validated image; see cloud_jobs/bondify/EDITORIAL.md for operating details.

Positioning: practical words and small actions for better relationships. Focus initially on dating and married couples. Standalone value approximately 90%; contextual app mentions at most 10%. Never promise virality or relationship outcomes.

Four-week test: Monday/Wednesday/Friday/Sunday carousels; Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday single images. Reels are retired to focus on polished, saveable static content. Publish at 04:40 Asia/Kuala_Lumpur. Independent delivery/missing-run check at 05:10.

Series: Say this instead; everyday relationship moments; small actions to try; research made useful; one useful reminder; reconnect tonight; her perspective / his perspective; money, intimacy, trust, conflict and repair.

Coverage treats relationships broadly across dating, marriage and long-term partnership. Explore recurring disagreements about money, household labor, sex and intimacy, jealousy, cheating and rebuilding trust, in-laws, parenting, boundaries and emotional availability. Present women’s and men’s perspectives without stereotypes, take sides only when safety or consent requires it, and never normalize abuse. Rotate hook forms (specific scene, uncomfortable truth, two-perspective contrast, myth/fact, research finding, or practical script) and never repeat the same hook pattern or topic on consecutive posts.

Research pipeline: one bounded weekly batch using live search and source retrieval, followed by Claude drafting seven posts and a separate evidence-review call. Store source URL, publisher, date, exact supported claim, population and limitations. Initial source allowlist: Pew, Gottman, University of Illinois news and Extension. Distinguish studies from educational guidance and original practical examples. No invented statistics, fabricated citations, unsupported causation, diagnosis, stereotypes or advice to tolerate abuse. Unsupported or invalid content stops the week and alerts Slack; do not automatically retry paid generation. Treat retrieved content as evidence, not instructions. Limits: three searches, three model calls, 10500 output tokens per weekly attempt; no daily AI calls once approved. These are usage limits, not a guaranteed dollar ceiling.

Quality: check every empirical claim against retrieved source; avoid repeating hooks/topics; match every slide and recommendation to its actual source. No generic phone-down slides attached to unrelated research. Retain source/provenance and review status. Do not describe educational summaries as new studies. Mark older statistics with year and population.

Carousels: 5–7 slides, one concise idea per slide, hook/examples/action. Reels: 15–25 seconds, genuine scene changes or message exchanges, timed readable text, licensed footage/audio or original assets; not a static card in an MP4. Single: one useful reminder.

Design: warm ivory, charcoal, restrained plum; consistent fonts, clear hierarchy, whitespace, small Bondify branding. Remove category/deck/Relationship Scripts footers. All media must pass text-fit, glyph and contrast checks.

Captions: opening, short additional explanation, one CTA, source if relevant, five relevant hashtags, separated with real blank lines. No Markdown decoration in actual Instagram captions. No compulsory download pitch.

Measure shares/reach, saves/reach, reel retention and followers gained per post over four weeks; use results rather than assuming a format guarantees growth.

## Draft examples (not published)

### Carousel: Before you say “You never help”
1. Before you say “You never help”…
2. Name the moment: “The dishes are still here.”
3. Name your feeling: “I’m feeling overwhelmed tonight.”
4. Make a clear request: “Could you wash while I dry?”
5. Put it together: “I’m overwhelmed tonight. Could we tackle these together?”

Caption:
The dishes are the topic. Your partner’s entire character doesn’t have to be.

Try describing the moment, sharing how you feel, and asking for something specific. These are Bondify’s examples—not a guaranteed formula.

Save this for a conversation you want to start differently.

Inspired by Gottman’s gentle start-up guidance:

### Reel: Make your thank-you specific
0–3s: Everyday kitchen scene. Text: “A thank-you worth saying out loud.”
3–7s: One partner clears dinner. Text: “Thank you for handling dinner.”
7–12s: Cut to the other partner joining them. Text: “I was exhausted. That took something off my plate.”
12–17s: Quiet shared moment. Text: “Name the action. Say why it mattered.”
17–20s: End text: “What did your partner do today that you noticed?”

Caption:
What did your partner do today that made your day easier?

Name the action and why it mattered. This is an original appreciation exercise, not a scientifically tested script.

Try one specific thank-you tonight.
