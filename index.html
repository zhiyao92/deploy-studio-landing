What it does

A checklist, not a maze of tabs

One screen per version, listing everything between you and App Review. Each row is a single tap from being fixed.

📋

Twelve rows, one screen

Build, export compliance, release notes, screenshots, store listing, app information, age rating, price, review contact, release option — and the purchases going with it.

💬

A Submit button that explains itself

When it's disabled, it tells you why in the words a person uses: “add at least one screenshot”, not a status enum.

🧾

Version and purchases together

App Review can take a version and its in-app purchases as one submission. Deploy Studio exposes that, so a release and the purchases it depends on go in together.

🔔

It tells you first

A Today screen answering “is there anything I should do” across every app, a notification when a version is approved or rejected, and a Home Screen widget.

📈

After the release

The App Store funnel — impressions, product page views, downloads and the conversion between them. Sales and proceeds. Ratings and reviews you can reply to.

🔧

The things that break a build

Certificates, profiles, devices and bundle IDs, plus TestFlight testers, phased release, sandbox testers, users and Xcode Cloud.

Your key never leaves the device

The .p8 lives in the device Keychain, locked to this device and never synced to iCloud. Tokens are signed on the device, and the only place they go is api.appstoreconnect.apple.com. There is no server, no account, and no copy of your data anywhere but your phone.

Nothing from your App Store Connect account — app names, bundle identifiers, sales figures, your key — is ever logged. The analytics the app does send are closed lists of screen names and counts, so your data can't end up in one even by mistake.
